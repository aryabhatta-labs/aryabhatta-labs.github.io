About Us

Making our world smarter, everyday.

Our Mission

Aryabhatta Labs is a technology startup focused on building AI-powered workflow applications that help professionals and companies succeed. We believe the boring, repetitive work should be handled by software and AI, freeing people to focus on more creative and meaningful endeavours.

Who We Are

A small, focused team of engineers and product builders based in Oakville, Ontario, working with clients and building our own products across AI, automation, and enterprise software.

What We Do

We consult on and build AI-enabled products — from expense and mileage tracking to LLM experimentation platforms — for teams that want to move fast without compromising on quality.

Our Values

Velocity without sacrificing quality, transparency with our customers, and a genuine curiosity about how AI can make everyday work simpler.
